Privacy Policy for FocusTrack

Last updated: 1 August 2026

FocusTrack (“the app”) is an Android application that measures how long you spend in each app on your device, groups those apps into categories, and shows you a daily focus score. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your information.

In short: FocusTrack does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. All information stays on your device. The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending your data anywhere.

Information the app accesses

To function, FocusTrack reads the following on your device only:

  • App usage statistics, via Android’s Usage Access special permission (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS). This covers which apps were in the foreground and for how long. You must grant this manually in Android Settings; the app does not work without it.
  • List of installed launchable apps, to display each app’s name and icon and to show which suggested apps you already have installed.
  • Your category choices, meaning any app you personally mark as productive, neutral, or distracting.

Information the app does NOT access

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or accounts. The app has no sign-in.
  • No content inside other apps: no messages, no browsing history, no URLs, no screen contents.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or calendar.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.

How the information is used

Usage data is used solely to render features you see in the app: your daily and weekly screen time, category breakdowns, trends, your focus score, the home-screen widget, and an optional daily summary notification. It is never used for profiling or advertising.

Data storage, sharing and retention

All data is stored locally on your device in the app’s private storage (an on-device database and app preferences). Because the app has no network access, nothing is uploaded to any server, and nothing is shared with the developer or any third party.

Daily totals are kept on-device so you can view your history. You can erase all stored data at any time by clearing the app’s storage (Settings → Apps → FocusTrack → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app, which permanently deletes everything.

You may also revoke Usage Access at any time in Settings → Apps → Special app access → Usage access, which stops all measurement.

Children’s privacy

FocusTrack is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Your data never leaves your device, which removes the risk of interception in transit or a server-side breach. On-device data is protected by Android’s standard app sandbox.
